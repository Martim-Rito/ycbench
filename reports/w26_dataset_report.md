## 5. Target construction

`target_predemo_score` re-implements the notebook (cells 12-20), not `scripts/scoring/yc_predemo_score.py`: **[F]** the script is a different method (robust z-scores over ARR/funding/GitHub/HN) and cannot run because `twitter_metrics.json` is absent from the repository. **[I]** The notebook is the version tied to the published result.

1. Base traction score = max(signal value x signal weight) over seven signal types; weights are read from notebook cell 12.
2. Velocity Score = base score x max(1 + 10 x MoM, 0.1); the floor belongs to the notebook implementation. Checked equal to `yc_w26_traction.csv.Velocity_Score` for all 11 rows.
3. Attention score = 0.05 x during-batch Google mentions (notebook cell 2 values).
4. `target_predemo_score` = max(Velocity Score, attention score); Velocity is 0 for the 193 startups without traction data. **[F]** their traction is *unobserved*, not zero: traction is unobserved for most startups, so the computed Pre-Demo Score may underestimate the score that would be obtained with complete traction coverage.
5. `target_top20` = 1 for the 20 highest scores among `target_defined = 1` rows, 0 for other defined rows, NaN for undefined rows. Ties broken by CSV domain. No tie at the rank 20/21 boundary (checked on the score values).
6. Score values duplicated among the 184 positive scores: 115 rows (the lowest top-20 score is not shared with a row outside the top 20); 14 rows tie at 0.

**Composition of the positives.** 11 come from the hand-entered Lobster Capital traction and 9 from during-batch Google mentions. **[F]** Every traction startup outranks every attention-only startup: True (lowest traction score vs highest attention-only score, computed). **[I]** The label therefore mostly encodes 'was covered by the article' plus 'Google visibility after 2026-01-01'.

**Sensitivity of the target to its input.** The committed `yc_mentions.csv` differs from the notebook's hard-coded during-batch values for 15 domains (14 are 0 in the notebook and positive in the CSV). Recomputing the target from `yc_mentions.csv` (NaN as 0) keeps only 12/20 of the positives. **[F]** This script follows the notebook, as it reproduces the committed `yc_w26_pre_demo_scores.csv`.

## 7. Future feature directions

Investigated in `scripts/pilot_w26_external_sources.py` (30-startup pilot, `reports/w26_pilot_acquisition_report.md`) and deliberately not used in v1. Cutoff 2025-10-31.

| direction | what the pilot showed | status |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn founder education / employment / previous founder experience | Most promising human-capital direction. P26 proxy (not W26): 205/229 founder profiles scraped (89.5%); education dated for 70-78% of entries; 54% of profiles show another founder role; YC entries (about 20% of education entries) must be masked. | Deferred: collection needs legal / ethics clearance, and point-in-time validation of entries. |
| Funding / investors | No funding or investor field exists anywhere in the repository for W26. | Future enrichment. |
| Network / ecosystem | Largely absent (Reddit run failed, no outputs). | Future enrichment. |
| Domain age (RDAP) | Retrieved 29/30. 10 of 29 CSV domains were registered after the cutoff (34%), so age is structurally absent for them; the domain is a 2026 website, registration after the cutoff is partly a consequence of acceptance, and it is mechanically tied to a target measured on that domain. 7 startups have different CSV/JSON domains (6 after normalising `try.wideframe.com`); old domains can carry a previous owner's history. | Deferred: needs a historical-identity rule, and a post-cutoff state cannot be imputed. |
| Certificate Transparency | 29/30 retrieved; 17/28 first certificates fall within 3 days of registration, 4/29 predate it (previous owner); about 10% transient failures. | Mostly redundant with domain age. |
| Wayback | Any capture by the cutoff 11/30; 5 of those 11 first captures predate the current registration (another owner). | Low / ambiguous coverage. |
| Hacker News | 1/30 stories and 1/30 comments matched. | Too sparse. |
| GitHub | Company-level repos by the cutoff 1/30; identity matching is weak. | Too sparse / identity issues. |
| Richer one-liner semantics / embeddings | Only the word count is used. | Deferred. |
| Additional historical web / domain reconstruction | See domain age, Wayback and CT. | Deferred. |

The earlier pilot recommendation to keep the earliest-registered of the CSV and JSON domains is **superseded**: it selects on the feature's own value and would make startups with two domains look older.
